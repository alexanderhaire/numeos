Support

Numeos · last updated 10 August 2026

Numeos is made by one person. Write to awhaire@gmail.com about anything at all — a bug, a puzzle whose answer looks wrong, a question about your account, or something someone has typed that should not be there. Every message is read by a human, and reports about names are read first.

Reporting a name on the leaderboard

Open Leaderboard, find the row, and tap the ⋯ button at the end of it. Two choices appear: Report name, which sends the name for review, and Block. Reporting is private — the person is not told who reported them, and the name is recorded as it stood at that moment, so renaming afterwards does not erase the evidence.

Reporting a group name

Open the group from Groups and tap Report this group at the bottom of its screen. A group name is seen by every member, which is why it is reportable by every member.

Blocking someone

Same place as reporting a name: Leaderboard → ⋯ → Block. That account disappears from your leaderboard immediately and stays gone. A block belongs to your account alone and changes nothing for anybody else, so nobody is told they have been blocked.

A group is a different relationship, because you chose to join it. The way out of one is Leave group on the group's own screen; you stop seeing it, and it stops seeing you.

If you cannot reach either of those

Both controls need you to be signed in, and the leaderboard only lists people who have chosen a display name. If what you want to report is not in front of you, use Profile → Report something, which opens an email to the address above, or write to it directly. Tell us what you saw and roughly where, and that is enough.

What happens to a report

Reports are read at least once a day. A display name or a group name that breaks the rules is removed within 24 hours of the report arriving, and an account that keeps doing it loses the ability to appear on the leaderboard. Names are also checked against a filter the moment they are set, so the obvious cases never reach anyone; the filter is not perfect, which is what reporting is for.

Numeos has no chat, no messaging, and no photo or file sharing. The only text one person can put in front of another is a display name and a group name, so those are the only two things there are to report.

Your account and your data

  • The app works without an account. Signing in with Apple is optional and adds the leaderboard and groups.
  • Profile → Account → Delete account, tapped twice to confirm, removes your account and its data immediately. There is no waiting period and no email to confirm.
  • Signing out leaves your progress on the phone. Deleting the app removes the copy on the phone and leaves the account alone.
  • What is stored, and where, is set out in the privacy policy.

Things that come up

  • Progress missing on a new phone. Sign in with the same Apple ID. Progress is carried by the account, not by the device, and the two copies are merged rather than one overwriting the other.
  • A name you want is refused. Display names are unique, two to eighteen characters, and a short list is reserved to stop impersonation. Try a variant.
  • A group is full. Thirty people is the limit, and it is enforced by the server rather than by the app.
  • You missed a Tuesday fixture. Fixtures are live and cannot be played late; that week scores zero and the season carries on.
  • An answer looks wrong. Send it — the puzzle, the answer shown, and what you expected. Puzzles are generated rather than stored, so a fault is usually a fault in a whole family of them and worth knowing about.
