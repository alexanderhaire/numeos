Privacy Policy

Numeos · last updated 18 August 2026

Numeos is a mental-arithmetic app. It works completely without an account, and it has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect anything about you. This policy describes the only data it handles.

If you never sign in

Nothing leaves your device. Your puzzles, streak, saved cards, daily goal and topic choices are stored locally on the phone and are not transmitted anywhere. There is no account, no identifier, and nothing for us to see. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

If you sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional. Its only purposes are to carry your progress to a new phone, to place you on the global leaderboard, and to let you belong to a group and play its league. Sign in with Apple is the only sign-in method offered, and Numeos never handles a password.

What is storedWhy
An account identifier from Apple, and the email address Apple returns To recognise the same account on another device. If you choose “Hide My Email”, Apple sends a relay address and that is all we ever receive.
A display name, if you choose one So you can appear on the global leaderboard. This is the only field other people can see, and it is shown only if you set one. You may change or clear it at any time.
Your progress: points, puzzles completed, how many you finished and scored on each day, the cards you saved, your daily goal, the topics you chose and whether sound is on So the same history and the same setup appear when you sign in on another device. This is the local state from the paragraph above, copied up so a new phone can have it back. Nobody else can read it.
A record of each quiz answered: which puzzle, on which day, how long it took, what it scored So scores can be totalled on the server rather than taken on trust from the app.
The groups you belong to, and the date you joined each one So a group can show its members, and so the league can work out who is playing on a match night.
The name, join code, difficulty and schedule of any group you create So other people can find it and join it. A group name is typed by whoever created the group and is visible to every member.
The time zone of the device that created a group So the season's ten fixtures are scheduled at the hour the group chose in one agreed place, rather than at a different hour for every member.
Each answer you give in a league fixture: which question, which option you chose, how long it took, and what it scored So the night can be scored on the server, and so the scoreboard can show how the group did.
Anything you report — the name as it stood, any reason you typed, and when — and the accounts you have blocked So reports can be reviewed, and so a blocked account stays hidden from your leaderboard. The name is copied rather than looked up later, so renaming after a report does not erase what was reported.

One part of the league is deliberately never stored: lobby chat. In the ten minutes before a match, the members of a group can talk in the lobby. Each message is checked against the same filter as names and then relayed live to the members present, and it is never written anywhere — not by Numeos and not by its hosting provider — so it cannot be read later, by us or by anyone. The only copy that survives is the text of a message you choose to report, which travels with your report and is kept as a moderation record.

That is the complete list. Numeos does not collect your name, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, health data, browsing activity, or anything else. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and nothing is shared with anyone beyond the hosting provider named below.

Who can see it

  • Your display name and total points appear on the global leaderboard, which any signed-in user can see. Nothing else about you appears there.
  • Your email address is never shown to anyone and never appears on the leaderboard.
  • Your solves in the daily feed are readable only by you.
  • Inside a group, the other members can see your display name, the date you joined, your total for the season, and the points you scored on each fixture night. That is what a league table is. They cannot see your email address, and they cannot see anything you do outside that group.
  • A group's name and its join code are visible to everyone in that group.
  • Reports you file are not visible to other users, including the person reported. Blocks are private to you.

Where it is stored

On Supabase, which hosts the database and authentication on our behalf. Data is transmitted over HTTPS and access is enforced per-account at the database level. Supabase acts only as our processor and does not use the data for its own purposes.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it. There is a Delete account control in the app under Account. It removes your account and everything stored against it — your profile and display name, your progress, your quiz records, your league answers, your group memberships, the accounts you had blocked and the reports you had filed — immediately and permanently. There is no waiting period, and no confirmation email is sent because there is nothing left to send it about.

Two things deliberately outlive the account, and it would be misleading not to say so. If somebody reported your display name, the report keeps that name as it stood when it was filed, because a moderation record that a rename can erase is not a record. And a group you created carries on under whoever is still in it, keeping the name you gave it — the alternative is that one person leaving destroys a season for twenty-nine others. A group whose last member leaves is deleted outright.

You can also simply sign out, which leaves your progress on the device and removes nothing from the server, or delete the app, which removes the local copy and leaves the account untouched.

Notifications

Reminders — before a match, or a daily puzzle at an hour you choose — are off until you turn them on. They are scheduled by the app on your phone itself: there is no push service, nothing is sent anywhere to make one fire, and your reminder settings stay on this device. Turning them off in the app or in iOS Settings ends them.

Children

Numeos is suitable for all ages and collects nothing beyond what is listed above. There is no photo or file sharing, and no messaging anywhere except one place: the ten-minute lobby before a group's match, where the members of that group — and nobody else — can talk. Lobby chat is filtered before it is relayed, never stored, and every message can be reported or its sender blocked on the spot.

Three pieces of text are written by users and seen by others: a display name, which appears on the global leaderboard and in group tables; a group name, which the members of that group see; and lobby chat, described above. All three are checked against a filter, all three can be reported from inside the app, and any account can be blocked so that its name no longer appears on your leaderboard and its messages no longer reach you You can leave a group at any time from the group’s own screen. Reports are read at the address below and offending names are removed by hand; there is no automated review, and no promise of a reply.

Your rights

You can see everything stored about you inside the app, change your display name at any time, and delete everything with the Delete account control. If you would prefer a copy of your data by email, or have any question about this policy, write to the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app.
