Numeos

A swipeable feed of mental-arithmetic puzzles, for iPhone.

Numeos deals an endless feed of mental-arithmetic problems, one card at a time. Tap a card to see the answer and the named trick behind it; every tenth card is a short test drawn from the nine you have just read. It works completely without an account. Signing in with Apple is optional and adds two things: a global leaderboard, and private groups of up to thirty people playing a ten-week league on Tuesday evenings.
